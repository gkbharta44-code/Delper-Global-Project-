1. The Global Problem & Macro Inefficiencies

  • The Global Logistics Bottleneck: Total global logistics spending commands between $11 Trillion and $13 Trillion annually , crippled by fragmented infrastructure and structural delays.
  • The $800 Billion Administrative Drain: Cross-border friction, manual customs compliance, and port demurrage waste up to $800 Billion in economic value each year.
  • Physical Sourcing Vulnerabilities: Industrial supply chains face severe cyber threats on legacy Operational Technology (OT), combined with a global shortage of qualified cyber-physical engineering talent.
  • Digital Commerce Vulnerabilities: Global B2B and retail e-commerce networks lose massive volumes to counterfeit goods, phantom inventory, and upfront capital exposure without independent quality validation.

2. The Integrated Solution: Four Initiatives, One Ledger

Delper Integrated merges four initiatives into a single economic engine, operating exclusively via the 100% fiat-backed ERC-20 Delper Token:

  • Project Iron Nexus ($40.00 Trillion): A global multimodal transport grid linking deepwater ports, dedicated electrified rail corridors, express highways, and high-velocity air cargo hubs. Rail freight is designed to cut transport energy use by 60%.
  • Project Make in India 🇮🇳 ($56.00 Trillion): A coastal software-defined manufacturing matrix in India. Approximately 560,000 dark factories on 1-acre coastal footprints are powered by 840 GW of dedicated zero-carbon nuclear baseload. Across a 7,500 km coastal corridor, these factories are projected to capture 28% of the world's physical manufacturing value-add. At 2024 world levels this is an after-tax profit of about $4.71 trillion per year, which is repaid to bondholders from the pooled net profit. Bharat starts after Iron Nexus is operative in Year 15: its profit begins in Year 19 and reaches full scale in Year 27.
  • Delper Aegis ($247.7 Billion ARR): An enterprise-tier industrial cybersecurity layer. Each production line sits behind an inline Delper Guard firewall targeting IEC 62443 Security Level 4 (SL-4), with a physical write-lock that blocks remote programming exploits.
  • Delper Global Trust Infrastructure: A buyer-first verified sourcing platform. Track A (Bulk) carries a 15.184% fixed transaction rate, and Track B (Retail) carries 24.2%. Both run on a 14-day settlement cycle.

14. Global Onboarding

Partners, operators, suppliers and team members joining the Delper Group across regions can start their onboarding once the Group confirms the sign-up route for their market. Onboarding steps are confirmed once the sign-up is complete.

Onboarding terms, eligibility and any regional requirements are set by the Group and the applicable jurisdiction. This link does not constitute an offer of securities or a financing commitment.

Parameter-based automation with human access

Delper's onboarding and sales processes run on defined parameters. Each step follows written rules and thresholds, such as eligibility criteria, document requirements, consent status and approved outreach templates. The system acts only within those parameters. Any case that falls outside them, or that the rules cannot decide, is routed to a person. Every automated process that makes or executes a decision affecting a partner, supplier, operator, team member or prospect has a named human owner, as set out in the Human Override Policy in Section 12.

At any point in onboarding or sales, an applicant, prospect or partner can choose to connect with a human. The request is routed to a named team member, and the person can pause or change the automated steps. Sign-up creates the partner or supplier record in Delper's own systems, and the sign-up route is to be confirmed by the Group.

In sales, AI searches Apollo for prospects that match a Delper service, within Apollo's terms and rate limits. The workflow drafts outreach only for prospects with a lawful basis for contact in their market. A salesperson approves each message before it is sent. Each message identifies Delper and includes an opt-out, and opt-outs are kept in Delper's own CRM and applied across all tools.

Parameters and human override

Each automated process has a named owner, a written set of parameters, a rule for escalating to a person, a control to halt the process, and an audit log of every escalation and override. Applicants and prospects can request a human review of any automated decision that affects them, at any stage.
