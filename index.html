EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Investment Opportunity

Delper Group is a dual-track digital commerce and security architecture backed by the 10-year market traction, infrastructure, and AAA-rated corporate client relationships of Radiant Synage Pvt Ltd. The group is organizing a total seed capital raise of USD 8.00 Million to scale two high-velocity trade and e-commerce platforms, culminating in a mega-scale Industrial Factory-as-a-Service (FaaS) rollout by Year 5.

Corporate Structure & Equity Allocation

The corporate framework is structured as a Consolidated Holding Company (Top-Co) controlling 100% of downstream operations, keeping Radiant Synage in operational and voting control while maximizing investor upside:

  • Total Capital Ask: USD 8.00 Million Cash (Split as two USD 4.00M allocations).
  • Total Implied Post-Money Valuation: USD 20.00 Million.
  • Radiant Synage Contribution: Appraised at USD 12.00 Million in corporate intellectual property (IP), banking lines, and infrastructure.
  • Equity Split: 60% Radiant Synage (Majority Control) / 40% Investor (Significant Minority).

Dual-Track Operational Architecture

Track A: Delper Global Consultants (Singapore SPV)

  • Focus: Cross-border B2B bulk enterprise trade platform utilizing Radiant's trust-layer integration model.
  • Capital Allocation (USD 4.0M): Trading capital of $0.86M (SAP BTP & Signavio middleware), spent set-up of $0.54M (SAP S/4HANA Core ERP $0.39M, SGS quality webhooks $0.15M), and a $2.60M pre-shipment margin (margin only, revolving and recycled on each FOB payment).
  • Unit Economics: Operates on a 15.184% fixed transaction rate over a rapid 14-day settlement loop (26 capital turns per year from Year 2), plus a 6.0% net annual liquidity spread on cargo-secured facilities. After-tax profit is split 40% to GK Bharta and 60% reinvested from Year 2.
  • Delivery: FOB terms for bulk sellers (seller delivers to origin port; buyer pays by LC).

Track B: Delper Global Retail (India Pilot, under Singapore SPV)

  • Focus: India-only pilot, buyer-first digital marketplace integrated with the Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC). Over 10 years the platform targets 1 billion products and 3 billion customers globally.
  • Seed Funding (USD 4.0M): Received in full on signing and spent per the use of funds (set-up and operations, not order funding), covering about 12 months of spending.
  • Unit Economics: Generates a 24.2% e-commerce take rate ($72.67 gross profit per baseline $300 order) driven by real-time factory bidding and viral QR-distributor onboarding.
  • Delivery: Doorstep delivery to retail buyers, with SGS as the quality control partner.

Investor Risk Mitigation: The 12-Month Exit Pipe

To completely de-risk the investor's USD 8.00 million cash commitment, Delper runs an isolated financial vehicle in parallel to the core technology tracks:

  • The Vehicle: Routes 1,000,000 metric tons of bulk palm oil annually at USD 15 per ton through Irrevocable At-Sight Letters of Credit (LCs), generating USD 15.00 Million of post-tax income a year.
  • Repayment Source: Palm oil post-tax income funds the Year 1 repayment of the USD 8.00 Million investor loans (11% interest, USD 0.88 Million) and the USD 4.00 Million Radiant payment.
  • Principal Return: 100% of the seed capital (USD 8.0M) is returned to the investor as a single lump-sum payout within 12 months. Net cash risk drops to USD 0.00 while the investor keeps their permanent 40% equity upside.

Long-Term Horizon: Delper Aegis Scaling

Cumulative post-tax income from Tracks A and B (excluding palm oil) funds the Aegis launch in Year 7:

  • Cumulative Track A and B post-tax income: about USD 1.14 Billion by Year 6 and about USD 3.70 Billion by Year 7.
  • Track B (Delper Retail) Year 10 net profit: USD 90.39 Billion (1 order per customer per year, 3 billion customers)
  • Combined Year 5 post-tax income from Tracks A and B: USD 243.68 Million (excludes palm oil)
  • Capital Deployment: USD 1.90 Billion Aegis launch capex, invested in Year 7 and funded from cumulative Track A and B profit. It rolls out 3,000,000 Delper Guard firewalls.
  • Model-based scale: Tracks A and B post-tax income reaches USD 91.25 Billion in Year 10 (Track A USD 0.86 Billion; Track B USD 90.39 Billion), which is the cash base the Aegis rollout draws on after its Year 7 launch.
