Executive Summary · Revised October 2026

Delper Group

A three-track digital trust, commerce and security group. From Year 1 GK Bharta holds 100% of the group and receives 40% of after-tax profit from each track every year, funded by palm oil income. Delper Aegis launches in Year 7.

Seed Round: USD 8.00M from Investor

Seed investors put USD 8.00 Million into the group, USD 4.00 Million into Track 1 (Global Trust Infrastructure) and USD 4.00 Million into Track B (Delper Retail). In Year 1 GK Bharta pays them USD 8.00 Million, which returns their principal and buys back their 40% interest. GK funds that payment from palm oil income. In Year 1 GK also acquires Radiant Synage's 60% interest for a lump-sum payment of USD 4.00 Million. From Year 1 GK holds 100% of the group and receives 40% of its after-tax profit each year, with 60% reinvested. In Year 7 the USD 1.90 Billion Delper Aegis launch is funded from GK Bharta's profit, and GK owns 100% of Aegis.

Dual-Track Operational Architecture

Track 1: Global Trust Infrastructure (Singapore SPV)

  • Focus: Cross-border B2B bulk enterprise trade platform utilizing Radiant's trust-layer integration model.
  • Initial capital (USD 4.0M, seed investors): USD 2.21M trading capital and USD 1.79M set-up, including SAP BTP and Signavio middleware ($0.86M), SAP S/4HANA Core ERP ($0.39M), and SGS quality webhooks ($0.15M).
  • Unit Economics: 15.184% take rate on premium commodities and 24.2% on consumer and high-margin goods, over a 14-day settlement cycle (26 capital turns per year from Year 2; 17.6 in Year 1).
  • Transactions must be at least USD 300. Liquidity spread: a 6.0% net annual spread on 14 to 90 day facilities secured by bonded cargo, which sits inside the 24.2% turnover basis and is not added on top.
  • Sovereign BOOT concessions from Year 6 apply 15.184% to port duties and clearing fees. These revenues are not included in the revenue figures in this summary.
  • Profit: operating expenses are 40% of revenue and income tax is 17% of pre-tax profit, leaving 49.8% of revenue as net profit. 40% of net profit is paid to GK Bharta each year; 60% is reinvested.

Track B: Delper Retail (India Domestic Engine)

  • Focus: India-focused, buyer-first digital marketplace integrated with the Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC).
  • Initial capital (USD 4.0M, seed investors): spent on the Year 1 pilot, covering core team and engineering, 3PQC set-up, escrow and licensing, RTB vetting, contingency and cloud.
  • The Retail model is unvalidated. Escrow with Axis Bank and QC with SGS are not yet signed. Sellers pay the 2% commission and lead generation from sale proceeds on escrow release, after a 15-day acceptance window.
  • Unit Economics: 2% commission on each sale, deducted from escrow release. Sellers pay a USD 100 annual onboarding fee and prepay real-time bidding and 3PQC inspection costs at actual cost. Buyers pay no separate fee.
  • Profit: revenue is Delper gross profit. Operating expenses are 50% and income tax is 17%, leaving 41.5% of revenue as net profit. 40% is paid to GK Bharta each year; 60% is reinvested.
  • Five-Year Target: 7.5M orders and buyers and 15,000 certified sellers in Year 5, India only through ONDC.

Delper Aegis (Launch in Year 7)

  • Focus: Factory-as-a-service industrial cybersecurity built around the Delper Guard firewall, with an initial hardware run of 3,000,000 units.
  • Funding: USD 1.90 Billion, funded from GK Bharta profit in Year 7, covering USD 531.5 Million of silicon development and USD 1,367.5 Million of hardware, a total launch cost of USD 1,899.0 Million.
  • Revenue starts in Year 7 at USD 5.91B and reaches USD 62.97B in Year 10 in the model.
  • Profit: operating costs follow the source cost schedule (FDE staffing, operations, firmware, cloud hosting and hardware cost of sales), with 17% tax on profit. GK advances the USD 1.90 Billion launch, which Aegis repays from its profit before the 40% GK share applies.
  • Certification testing runs in Years 7 to 8.

Seed Round Buyback by GK, Funded by Palm Oil

The palm oil vehicle routes 1,000,000 metric tons of bulk palm oil a year through irrevocable at-sight letters of credit. GK Bharta earns a sourcing commission of USD 15 per ton, irrespective of palm oil price, which gives USD 15.00 Million a year. In Year 1 GK uses this income to pay the seed investors USD 8.00 Million, which returns their principal and buys back their 40% interest, and to pay Radiant USD 4.00 Million. The palm oil business continues as usual after the buyback. Malaysia is used only for palm oil purchasing, to clear the debt.

Delper Aegis: Factory-as-a-Service, 100% Capital from GK

Delper Aegis is an industrial-grade cybersecurity layer built around the Delper Guard firewall, with an initial hardware run of 3,000,000 units. The USD 1.90 Billion launch in Year 7 is advanced from GK Bharta's profit share, covering USD 531.5 Million of silicon development and USD 1,367.5 Million of hardware, a total launch cost of USD 1,899.0 Million. Revenue starts in Year 7.

  • Certification: IEC 62443 SL-4 is a target, not a rating. Formal testing is scheduled for program Years 1 to 2, which is Years 7 to 8 in the group timeline.
  • Roadmap, in group years from the Year 7 launch: launch and certification in Years 7 to 8; AI assurance in Years 9 to 11; post-quantum migration in Years 12 to 13; sustained lifecycle support in Years 14 to 16.
  • Full-scale recurring revenue: about USD 188.5 Billion a year at 1,000,000 lines on the revised bill of USD 15,706.50 a month per line. This is a gross projection that assumes every line is staffed and billed. If customers accept only the USD 8,371.50 source subscription, it falls to about USD 100.5 Billion. The group model uses the supply-limited revenue path instead: USD 5.91B in Year 7, rising to USD 62.97B in Year 10.
  • Workforce: round-the-clock cover needs 4.2 field engineers (FDEs) per plant. At 12 lines per plant, the full 1,000,000-line base needs about 350,000 FDEs. The source's talent model supports about 159,390 engineers, so hiring capacity, not customer demand, is the binding constraint. The 33,000 figure is the market shortage of FDE roles, not Aegis's own requirement.
  • Funding and payout: GK Bharta advances the full Year 7 launch from its profit share. Aegis repays that advance from its own after-tax profit before any profit split. Only the remaining profit is paid out, 40% to GK Bharta and 60% reinvested. On the source cost schedule, the advance is repaid within Year 7. If Year 7 revenue fell below about USD 4.4 Billion, the advance would carry into Year 8.
  • Verdict: conditionally viable. It requires plants averaging about 26 or more lines, customers accepting a total bill near USD 15,707 per line per month, and full funding of the launch.

GK Bharta Cash Flow, Years 1 to 10

This section sets out GK's cash position and the ten-year financial waterfall. GK's cash comes from palm oil income of USD 15.00 Million a year, from 1,000,000 metric tons at USD 15 per ton, and from 40% of after-tax profit paid by each track. Each track reinvests 60% of after-tax profit. In Year 1 GK pays the seed investors USD 8.00 Million and Radiant USD 4.00 Million. Delper Aegis is funded entirely from GK's profit share until it can fund itself. Only the remaining profit is split 40% to GK and 60% reinvested. The advance is repaid in Year 7, so Aegis's payout starts that year. Cash stays positive in every year under the revised model, so no fresh investment is required. Figures are in USD.

Project Iron Nexus: GK Bharta Bond Purchases from Year 15

Project Iron Nexus is a $40.00 Trillion global trade infrastructure program built over a 15-year construction horizon. GK Bharta takes part by buying program bonds from Year 15, funded from its 40% share of after-tax profit from Track 1, Track B and Delper Aegis. No bond purchases are made before Year 15. The bonds are bought to build a multimodal global supply chain, linking deepwater ports, dedicated rail freight corridors, express highways, air cargo hubs and digital customs systems.
